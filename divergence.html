BTC RSI · Divergence Tracker

RSI-14 divergences on BTC 1h & 2h (Kraken): the backtested 3-pivot pattern, plus 2-pt single-leg pairs filtered to RSI extremes (first pivot ≥65 / ≤35) — unvalidated, tracked to build a live sample. Pivots need 3 confirming bars, so alerts fire ~3 candles after the final pivot prints. Trades are manual — the monitor only alerts (Telegram) and tracks each signal 48h forward. All returns are in the trade's direction (bearish = short), excluding fees.
